隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 29 日

FC Emulator Library 是在 Apple TV 上运行的本地游戏资料库和模拟器应用。应用不包含广告、跨应用跟踪、分析服务或由开发者运营的账户服务。开发者不会收集你的个人数据、资料库内容、游戏记录、ROM 文件、封面、存档、密码或访问令牌。

应用处理的数据

  • 资料库地址、显示偏好、轻量索引和游戏进度在你的 Apple TV 上处理。
  • 密码和访问令牌保存在 Apple Keychain 中。
  • 下载的 ROM、封面、元数据及临时同步文件保存在应用的 tvOS 沙盒内。可重新下载的数据使用缓存目录，可能被 tvOS 清理。
  • 如果你配置 HTTP(S) 或 SMB 资料库，应用只会直接连接你填写的服务器。局域网发现和浏览器辅助配置仅用于连接或配置你自己的资料库。
  • 存档同步是可选功能，仅对你配置的兼容 HTTP(S) 资料库启用。存档直接在 Apple TV 与该服务器之间传输；SMB 资料库不会上传存档。

你选择的服务器运营者可能按照自己的隐私规则处理请求。FC Emulator Library 不会把这些数据发送到由应用开发者运营的服务器。

共享与跟踪

应用不会向开发者或广告公司出售、共享个人数据，不会跨应用或网站跟踪用户，也不会将数据用于定向广告。

保留与删除

你可以控制应用保存的数据。在“设置 → 存储与缓存”中选择“清理全部游戏”，即可从应用中删除游戏、缓存、存档、资料库地址和已保存的认证信息。卸载应用也会删除其本机数据。保存在你自己的 NAS 或 HTTP(S) 服务器上的数据，需要在对应服务器上删除。

儿童隐私

应用不会有意收集儿童或其他用户的个人信息。

政策变更与联系

重要变更会同步更新到本文档及应用内的隐私政策页面。

English

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

FC Emulator Library is a local game-library and emulator app for Apple TV. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a developer-operated account service. The developer does not collect your personal data, library contents, play history, ROM files, artwork, save data, passwords, or access tokens.

Data processed by the app

  • Library addresses, display preferences, lightweight indexes, and game progress are processed on your Apple TV.
  • Passwords and access tokens are stored in the Apple Keychain.
  • Downloaded ROMs, artwork, metadata, and temporary synchronization files are stored inside the app's tvOS sandbox. Rebuildable files use the app's cache directory and may be removed by tvOS.
  • If you configure an HTTP(S) or SMB library, the app connects directly to the server address you provide. Local-network discovery and browser-assisted setup are used only to connect to or configure your own library.
  • Save synchronization is optional and available only for a compatible HTTP(S) library you configure. Save data travels directly between your Apple TV and that server. SMB libraries do not upload save data.

The operator of a server you choose may process requests according to their own privacy practices. FC Emulator Library does not send this data to a server operated by the app developer.

Sharing and tracking

The app does not sell or share personal data with the developer or advertising companies, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use data for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

You control the data stored by the app. In “Settings → Storage & Cache,” choose “Clear All Games” to remove games, caches, save data, library addresses, and saved authentication information. Uninstalling the app also removes its on-device data. Data stored on a NAS or HTTP(S) server you control must be deleted on that server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this document and in the in-app privacy-policy page.
