Three steps

A short setup.
Your files stay yours.

  1. 01

    Download and install

    Android TV: send the APK to your TV and install it with a file manager or ADB. When prompted, allow installation from that source. macOS: open the DMG and drag the app to Applications. The download is signed and notarized by Apple.

  2. 02

    Connect your own library

    On Mac, import and organize local games in ROM Manager without a server, or connect to your Synology library in Settings. Read the macOS setup guide →

  3. 03

    Connect a controller and play

    Use a Bluetooth or USB controller. Hold the confirm button on a game to open its options, add it to favorites, manage its cache, or view game details.
