Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Retro Console is a local game-library and emulator app for Android TV, Apple TV, and macOS. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a developer-operated account service. The developer does not collect your personal data, library contents, play history, game files, artwork, save data, passwords, or access tokens.

Data processed by the app

  • Library addresses, display preferences, lightweight indexes, and game progress are processed only on your device.
  • Passwords and access tokens are stored in Android Keystore or Apple Keychain.
  • Game files you import, along with artwork, metadata, and temporary synchronization files, stay inside the app sandbox. Rebuildable cache files may be removed by the operating system.
  • If you configure an HTTP(S) or SMB library, the app connects only to the server address you provide.
  • Save synchronization is optional and available only for a compatible HTTP(S) library you configure. Save data travels directly between the client and that server. SMB libraries do not upload save data.

The operator of a server you choose may process requests according to their own privacy practices. Retro Console Library does not send this data to a server operated by the app developer.

The game included with the app, Controller Quest: Flight Drill, is an original work by the developer. It does not connect to the network and collects no data.

Sharing and tracking

The app does not sell or share personal data with the developer or advertising companies, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use data for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

You control the data stored by the app. In “Settings → Storage & Cache,” choose “Clear All Games” to remove games, caches, save data, library addresses, and saved authentication information. Uninstalling the app also removes its on-device data. Data stored on a NAS or HTTP(S) server you control must be deleted on that server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this document and in the in-app privacy-policy page.
